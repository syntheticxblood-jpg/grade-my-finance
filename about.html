Aaron
Creator, Grade My Finance

Why I made this

Before this existed, I was just typing my own numbers into an AI chatbot every so often to see how I was doing. It worked okay, but it wasn't a real tool — just a workaround. I wanted something simpler: one place to check in, get a real grade, and see exactly what to work on next.

A bit about me

I don't work in finance — it's just something I'm genuinely into. I check my own numbers all the time, and this site is basically that habit turned into something other people (and some financial advisors, for their clients) can use too.

What I hope you get out of it

Even a bad grade should teach you something. The goal isn't a perfect score — it's understanding where you stand and knowing the next step to get better.
