The Opportunity

Clients want clarity. Most tools give them spreadsheets.

A letter grade is something every client already understands, instantly — no financial literacy required to know that a C+ needs work and an A- is worth celebrating. That's the entire hook, and it's why this format engages people who'd otherwise never open a budget worksheet.

A–F

A grading scale everyone already understands from school — zero learning curve for your clients.

1 page

The entire assessment fits on one screen: grade, breakdown, and a specific path to improve it.

Your brand

Every report card carries your firm's name and colors — clients associate the clarity with you.

Security & Compliance

Nothing to explain to your compliance team

Every calculation runs entirely in the client's own browser. There's no backend database of client financial figures for anyone to worry about protecting, because one was never built.

✓

No client financial data stored, ever

Income, debt, and asset figures are calculated locally and never transmitted to any server.

✓

No client accounts or passwords

Nothing for your clients to sign up for, remember, or for your firm to secure.

✓

Aggregate reporting only

Your firm can see anonymized, aggregate usage patterns — never an individual client's actual figures.

✓

Transparent, published methodology

The scoring logic isn't a black box — every factor and weighting is disclosed plainly to end users.

✓

No bank account linking

Unlike account-aggregation tools, nothing ever connects to a client's actual financial institution.

✓

Educational framing throughout

Every report clearly states this is an educational tool, not individualized financial advice — keeping your firm's own advice the clear point of value.

Questions Advisors Ask

Straight answers, before you ask

Is this meant to replace what I actually do for clients?

No — it's positioned explicitly as an educational starting point, not individualized advice. It gives clients a simple, honest first read on where they stand, which tends to make the real conversation with you more focused and productive, not less necessary.

How long does white-label setup actually take?

For the Firm tier, typical setup is a short back-and-forth to confirm branding assets (logo, colors) and a subdomain, followed by a review before going live. We'll walk through exact timing on a call.

Can we adjust the scoring methodology for our client base?

The core methodology is standardized and transparently published for consistency and trust. Enterprise partners with a specific, well-justified need can discuss adjustments directly.

What do we actually see on our end?

Aggregate, anonymized usage patterns only — total completions, average grades, common weak points across your client base. Never an individual client's specific financial figures, by design.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

Terms are discussed directly based on your firm's size and tier — we're happy to start with a shorter trial period for firms who want to test it with a subset of clients first.
